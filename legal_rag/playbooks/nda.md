# Playbook: NDA (mutual or one-way)

DRAFT: positions are starting points for a Malaysian SME and need a lawyer's review before relying on them. Items marked ⚠ must be verified.

## Payment terms
Not applicable to a standard NDA. Flag any fee or deposit clause for the owner.
- Walk-away: any penalty payable just for signing.

## Late payment
Not applicable. Flag if present.

## Liability cap
- Preferred: each party liable for actual proven loss from a breach; no cap on deliberate disclosure.
- Fallback: cap at a stated RM figure, never below the value of the deal.
- Walk-away: one-sided unlimited liability on us only.
- Why: the Contracts Act 1950 s.74 gives compensation for loss that naturally arose; a stated sum is only recoverable as reasonable compensation, not as a pure penalty.

## Indemnity
- Preferred: none.
- Fallback: indemnity limited to third-party claims caused by our own breach.
- Walk-away: indemnity for the other side's own legal costs on any dispute.
- Why: open-ended indemnities turn a free document into a big liability.

## Termination
- Preferred: either side ends on 30 days written notice; confidentiality survives.
- Fallback: fixed term of 2 years, then ends.
- Walk-away: no way to end it at all.

## Auto-renewal
- Preferred: no auto-renewal.
- Fallback: renews yearly with a 30-day opt-out reminder.
- Walk-away: silent renewal with a notice window under 30 days.

## IP ownership
- Preferred: each side keeps its own IP; nothing is licensed beyond the stated purpose.
- Walk-away: wording that gives the other side ownership of ideas we share.

## Confidentiality
- Preferred: defines "Confidential Information"; excludes public information, independently developed information and information already known; 3 years after end (trade secrets: as long as they stay secret).
- Fallback: 5 years flat.
- Walk-away: "everything" is confidential with no exceptions and no end date.
- Why: a clause with no limit is hard to obey and hard to enforce.

## Restraint of trade
- Preferred: no non-compete or non-solicit inside an NDA.
- Walk-away: any clause stopping us from trading or hiring.
- Why: Contracts Act 1950 s.28 makes agreements in restraint of trade void (with narrow exceptions). ⚠ lawyer to confirm.

## Governing law
- Preferred: laws of Malaysia.
- Fallback: neutral law (Singapore or England) only for foreign counterparties.
- Walk-away: a foreign law and court that we cannot afford to use.

## Dispute resolution
- Preferred: Malaysian courts after 14 days of good-faith talks. For small amounts, note small claims routes (Rules of Court 2012, Order 93 ⚠ current limit).
- Fallback: mediation, then court.
- Walk-away: arbitration seated overseas with the other side choosing the arbitrator.

## Personal data in the NDA
- If personal data will be shared, add a PDPA 2010 clause: use only for the purpose, keep secure, delete or return at the end, report a breach promptly.
