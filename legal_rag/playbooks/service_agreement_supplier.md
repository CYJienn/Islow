# Playbook: Service agreement (we are the SUPPLIER of goods or services)

DRAFT: positions are starting points for a Malaysian SME and need a lawyer's review. Items marked ⚠ must be verified.

## Payment terms
- Preferred: 30% deposit, balance within 14 days of invoice.
- Fallback: net 30 days from invoice.
- Walk-away: net 60 days or more, or payment "when the customer is paid".
- Why: cash flow. Old unpaid debts also expire: Limitation Act 1953 gives 6 years for contract claims (s.6 ⚠ verify) so recover early.

## Late payment
- Preferred: 1.5% per month on overdue amounts, plus right to pause service after 14 days.
- Fallback: a fixed rate of interest agreed up front.
- Walk-away: no late payment clause at all.
- Why: a clause is enforceable as reasonable compensation, not as a penalty (Contracts Act 1950 s.74). Keep the rate modest and explain it.

## Liability cap
- Preferred: our liability capped at the fees paid in the last 12 months; no liability for indirect or lost profit.
- Fallback: cap at the total contract value.
- Walk-away: unlimited liability.
- Why: one bad job should not close the company.

## Indemnity
- Preferred: we indemnify only for third-party IP claims over our own work.
- Fallback: mutual indemnities, each capped at the liability cap.
- Walk-away: we indemnify for anything the customer chooses to do with our work.

## Termination
- Preferred: either side with 30 days notice; immediate if the other side commits a serious breach and fails to fix it in 14 days; customer pays for work done to date.
- Walk-away: customer can end for convenience with no payment for work done.

## Auto-renewal
- Preferred: renews for the same term unless either side gives 60 days notice. Diarise the notice date.
- Fallback: renews monthly.
- Walk-away: renewal by us staying silent where the price can rise without our consent.

## IP ownership
- Preferred: we keep our tools and know-how; customer owns the final deliverable only after full payment.
- Fallback: customer owns the deliverable on delivery, but pays a licence fee for anything built from our pre-existing work.
- Walk-away: customer owns everything we create, including our existing tools.

## Confidentiality
- Preferred: mutual, 3 years after the end.
- Walk-away: one-sided and with no end.

## Governing law
- Preferred: laws of Malaysia.
- Walk-away: foreign law with no Malaysian forum.

## Dispute resolution
- Preferred: written notice, 14 days of talks, then mediation, then Malaysian courts. Use the small claims procedure for small sums (Order 93 ⚠ current limit).
- Walk-away: arbitration seated overseas.

## Personal data
- If we handle customer personal data, a PDPA 2010 clause (purpose limit, security, breach notice) is required. The 2024 amendment adds breach notification duties ⚠ verify commencement.
