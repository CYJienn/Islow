# Playbook: Supplier / purchase agreement (we are the BUYER)

DRAFT: positions are starting points for a Malaysian SME and need a lawyer's review. Items marked ⚠ must be verified.

## Payment terms
- Preferred: net 45 days from a correct invoice.
- Fallback: net 30 days.
- Walk-away: payment in full up front for goods not yet delivered, with no refund right.
- Why: we should only pay against delivery and a correct invoice. Check the bank account on every invoice against the one in the contract (fraud check).

## Late payment
- Preferred: no interest, or interest only after a written reminder and 14 days of grace.
- Fallback: modest simple interest.
- Walk-away: a flat fixed penalty on any day of delay.
- Why: Contracts Act 1950 s.74 allows only reasonable compensation, not a penalty.

## Liability cap
- Preferred: supplier's liability not capped below the contract value; no cap for defective goods that harm customers.
- Fallback: cap at 2x the annual fees.
- Walk-away: supplier liability capped at a token amount (for example RM1,000).
- Why: a food or product defect can cost far more than one order.

## Indemnity
- Preferred: supplier indemnifies us for product defects, recalls and IP claims.
- Fallback: indemnity limited to the liability cap.
- Walk-away: we indemnify the supplier for how we resell the goods.

## Termination
- Preferred: we may end on 30 days notice; immediately for repeated late or defective delivery.
- Walk-away: we are locked in for the full term with no exit.

## Auto-renewal
- Preferred: no auto-renewal.
- Fallback: renews yearly; notice window 60 days or less; reminder diarised 90 days before. See profile contract C-001 (notice deadline matters).
- Walk-away: auto-renewal with price increases and a notice window we cannot realistically meet.

## IP ownership
- Preferred: our brand, specs and packaging designs remain ours; supplier gets no licence beyond making our order.
- Walk-away: supplier owns designs made for our brand.

## Confidentiality
- Preferred: mutual, 3 years.
- Walk-away: supplier may share our pricing and customers.

## Governing law
- Preferred: laws of Malaysia.
- Walk-away: foreign law with no Malaysian forum.

## Dispute resolution
- Preferred: talks, mediation, then Malaysian courts.
- Fallback: arbitration seated in Kuala Lumpur.
- Walk-away: arbitration where the supplier chooses the arbitrator and the seat is overseas.

## Quality, delivery and inspection
- Preferred: right to inspect, reject and get a refund or replacement within 7 days of delivery.
- Walk-away: goods deemed accepted on delivery.
