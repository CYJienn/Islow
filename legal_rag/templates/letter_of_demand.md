## Checklist before sending
- Debt is within the limitation period.
- Amount, invoice number and dates match the records.
- The contract allows interest or costs before you claim them.
- Owner approved (amount is above the approval limit in the company profile?).
- Attach copies of the invoice, contract and reminders.
