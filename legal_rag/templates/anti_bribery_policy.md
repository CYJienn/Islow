# Template: Anti-bribery and corruption policy

DRAFT template. MACC Act 2009 s.17A makes a company liable when someone associated with it gives a bribe for its benefit; having adequate procedures is the defence. Align with the MACC Guidelines on Adequate Procedures (TRUST principles). ⚠ verify against the Guidelines. Not legal advice.

## Policy statement
{{company_name}} does not offer, give, ask for or accept bribes in any form, anywhere, for any reason. The owner and management are committed to this policy and will not punish anyone for refusing a bribe.

## Scope
Applies to owners, directors, staff, and anyone acting for us (agents, suppliers, contractors).

## Prohibited
- Offering or accepting money, gifts, favours, jobs or entertainment to influence a decision.
- Facilitation payments to speed up a government service.
- Using a third party to do what we cannot do ourselves.

## Gifts and hospitality
Allowed only if small, rare, open, and not linked to a decision. Gifts above RM{{gift_limit}} must be approved in writing and logged. Never give or accept cash.

## Dealing with officials
All payments to government bodies must be official fees with a receipt. Licence or permit fees above RM{{approval_limit}} need owner approval.

## Risk assessment (T and R of TRUST)
The owner reviews corruption risk every {{months}} months: which staff meet officials, which suppliers are in high-risk areas, and which payments are unusual.

## Controls
Two-person approval for payments above the owner approval limit. Check the bank account on every invoice. Keep a gift and hospitality register. Check suppliers before engaging them.

## Training and communication
Every new hire reads this policy. Yearly refresher for all staff. Suppliers receive a copy.

## Reporting
Report concerns to {{reporting_contact}} or anonymously via {{channel}}. No retaliation. Serious cases may be reported to the MACC.

## Review
Reviewed each year, and after any incident. Version {{version}}, approved by {{owner}} on {{date}}.
