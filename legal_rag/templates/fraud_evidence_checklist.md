# Checklist: Fraud evidence preservation

Use right after a suspected fraud (for example a changed bank account on an invoice). Related offences: Penal Code s.405-409 (criminal breach of trust) and s.415-420 (cheating). Not legal advice.

## Do first (same day)
- Stop further payments to the account or person involved.
- Call the bank to ask for a recall or hold of the payment; get the reference number and time of the call.
- Do not confront the suspect. Do not delete anything.

## Preserve
- The invoice and the original PDF or email with full headers (not just a screenshot).
- The earlier, genuine invoice or contract showing the correct bank account.
- All emails, WhatsApp chats and call logs about the change; export them with dates.
- Bank transfer slip, statement line and the bank's reply.
- Accounting entries and who approved the payment (approval log).
- Names, phone numbers and email addresses of everyone involved.

## Protect
- Make copies in a separate folder with read-only access; note who handled them and when.
- Change passwords and turn on two-step login for email and online banking if an account may be compromised.
- Write a timeline: date, time, what happened, who, source document.

## Then
- File a police report (see police_report_checklist).
- Brief a lawyer (see lawyer_brief).
- Tell the owner. Tell the genuine supplier by a phone number you already had on file.
