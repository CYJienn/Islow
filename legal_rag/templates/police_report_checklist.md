# Checklist: Police report (commercial fraud)

Go to the nearest police station or the Commercial Crime Investigation Department (CCID) office. Not legal advice. ⚠ confirm the current procedure with the station.

## Bring
- Company SSM certificate and the reporter's NRIC and authority letter from the owner.
- Printed timeline (date, time, event, amount).
- Printed evidence from the fraud evidence checklist: invoice, bank slip, statements, chats and emails, the genuine contract.
- Suspect details: name, phone number, bank account number, bank name, email, website.

## State clearly in the report
- Who the company is and who you are.
- What happened, in date order, in your own words. Facts only; no guessing.
- Amount lost, how it was paid, and the bank reference.
- What you did after (bank call, stopped payments).
- That you want an investigation and a copy of the report.

## After
- Keep the report number and a copy; give it to the bank and your lawyer.
- Note the investigating officer's name and contact.
- Update the matter file and the owner.
